# Demo 5 — Two-period product lines → slope

Exactly two periods, several entities, “who rose / who fell / who is steepest.” Now the skill should switch to **坡度圖** — proving it does not always say dumbbell.

## Paste this

```
產品線回顧。只要 2025 vs 2026 兩年營收（百萬新台幣），看誰升誰降、誰最陡。示意未核。中間年份不重要。

| 產品線 | 2025 | 2026 |
|--------|------|------|
| 訂閱雲 | 120 | 168 |
| 硬體配件 | 90 | 72 |
| 顧問專案 | 55 | 61 |
| 教育授權 | 40 | 38 |
| 通路白牌 | 70 | 95 |

請推薦圖種（資料在說什麼格式），並說明何時該改啞鈴或凹凸圖。結論句用台灣報表語氣。
```

## You should hear

- **推薦：坡度圖（slope chart）** — 實體 × 恰好兩期，故事是軌跡與斜率.
- **備選：** 啞鈴 if the ask becomes「每條產品線兩年差多少」as a ranked gap; 凹凸圖 if they switch to *rank* over more periods.
- Left/right same scale; optional up/down color; 未核; 百萬新台幣.
- `examples/slope-two-period.md`.
- Takeaway like：`訂閱雲與通路白牌在 2025–2026 上升最陡，硬體配件下降（未核）。`

## Why it wows

Paired with demo 4, this shows the skill can tell **gap vs trajectory**. That judgment is the product.
